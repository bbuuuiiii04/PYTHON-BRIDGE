# Bridge Cues Timing & Engine Configurations

This document summarizes the internal engine configurations for all Realtime Cues, including beat timings, sync modes, and supported parameters.

### `buildup_freestyle_nebula`
- **Description & Timing:** 32-beat freestyle: opposite comets + breathing purple/magenta bg + shutter strobe + climax flash.
- **Sync Mode:** `continuous`
- **Beat Division:** `1.0`
- **Supported Params:** `beat_division, duration_beats, heads, max_pulses, reverse, spawn_on_wrap, sync_mode, trail_beats, travel_beats, width`
