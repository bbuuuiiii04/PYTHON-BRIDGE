3 · Zones & palettes

When a track loads, the room reads its character and picks one of seven color worlds. Two knobs decide it: how aggressive it is, and how bright it is (plus distortion for the red zone).

GLACIER

Bright + clean, not aggressive. Airy, icy, melodic tracks land here.

Chosen when: aggression < 0.42 and luminance ≥ 0.40

Laser colors: blue, cyan, white

Tracks that land here: STARsound pt3

DEEP_POOL

Dark + clean, low glow. Moody, deep, sub-heavy but not distorted.

Chosen when: aggression < 0.42 and luminance < 0.28

Laser colors: blue, cyan

Tracks that land here: ILL, Can't Say Nah, Satisfaction, Caramelle

TWILIGHT

Mid-dark, purple dusk. In-between energy — not bright, not pitch-black.

Chosen when: aggression < 0.42 and luminance 0.28–0.40

Laser colors: blue, purple

Tracks that land here: no example among the 10 anchors

ION

Bright + aggressive. Punchy, energetic, luminous — the loud pretty stuff.

Chosen when: aggression ≥ 0.42 and luminance ≥ 0.52

Laser colors: green, cyan

Tracks that land here: Cruel Summer, Hide and Seek, FE!N

VOLT

Aggressive but darker than ION. Hard and electric without the brightness.

Chosen when: aggression ≥ 0.42 and luminance < 0.52 (not distorted)

Laser colors: purple

Tracks that land here: no example among the 10 anchors

EMBERCORE

Aggressive + distorted. The gritty red monster zone — growl and heat.

Chosen when: aggression ≥ 0.42 and distortion ≥ 0.75

Laser colors: red, white

Tracks that land here: DROP EM, kidstopbreathing

NEUTRAL

The safe fallback when a track doesn't read strongly as anything.

Chosen when: thin / ambiguous analysis — no strong read

Laser colors: blue, cyan

Tracks that land here: no example among the 10 anchors

4 · The energy system

Inside a zone, every drop gets typed by family and tier, and the moment right before it gets a darkness treatment. This is the brain that fires tonight.

Families — what kind of drop it is

WALL

Distorted / heavy / aggressive. Trap monsters and gritty walls of sound.

HARD

COMET

Fast, dark, pounding. High-BPM, no air, deep sub, relentless.

HARD

HOUSE

Groovy, clean, danceable. 116–144 BPM swing and stabs.

soft

NEUTRAL

Soft, melodic, or ambiguous. The catch-all when nothing reads strongly.

soft

WALL & COMET are the "hard" families — only they can earn the biggest 16-beat blackout, and they drive the lasers hardest.

Tiers — how violent that drop is

Tier 1standard
Tier 2intense
Tier 3monster

A "violence" score (loudness, punch, attack, density, gap) sorts drops into 1/2/3 against the whole library. Safety catch: a drop in the first 64 beats of a track is held to tier 1 — an intro can't fire a monster.

The darkness ladder — the moment before the drop

This is tier-independent: length is set by the family and how the build collapses, not the tier. The measured gap always caps the length, and if the low end comes back early the dark releases early.

snap
music slams straight in
1
tiny cut / short pickup
2
soft groove runs in
4
hard drop, or drums driving the build
8
true stop: drums done, vocals/FX still audible
16
true collapse into a WALL/COMET monster — hard families ONLY
balloon

Melodic swell (no drums, not a vocal stop) — the light shrinks down instead of going black.

dip

Full band ducks but the floor stays on — a ~4-beat dim, not a black.

Two different darknesses — don't mix them up. The ladder above is the LED room going dark right before a drop. Separately, the lasers now have their own move: a 4-beat laser blackout right before each chorus phrase (new tonight, AWR-170) — the LEDs stay up, only the lasers cut, then the chorus lands.

White share

The plan measures how hard the build rises and computes how much white should mix into the peak (brighter build → whiter top, from 25% up to 100%). Honest note: this value is computed and calibrated but not yet wired to the LEDs — it's a tuning input waiting on a live pass, not something you'll see change tonight.

What the lasers do per tier (the energy gate)

Drop reads as…Lasers
NEUTRAL (any tier)small — lasers stay SILENT
tier 1standard
tier 2intense
tier 3monster

The zone picks the laser color menu; the tier picks how hard it chases. The red+white (EMBERCORE) menu splits harder as energy climbs — chase #100 standard → #116 intense → #140 monster (that's tonight's AWR-170).

7 · The USB stick

A self-contained “RBSS Bridge.app” on a stick that runs the whole lighting brain on a Mac with no install.

Where it is tonight: the build phase opened and was proven the same night — a ~30 MB app, ad-hoc signed, packed into a ~111 MB DMG; the frozen smoke tests passed.

Build / refresh the stick

  1. PyInstaller builds the app from packaging/rbss_launcher.spec.
  2. packaging/sign.sh signs it — a real Apple cert if one exists, otherwise ad-hoc (it never blocks).
  3. hdiutil wraps it into the DMG. A rebuild is just re-running these same scripts.

First run on someone else’s Mac

  1. Right-click the app → Open to clear Gatekeeper. [how macOS normally behaves for an ad-hoc, non-notarized app — not written down as a step, so assume it.]
  2. Grant the one-time permissions it asks for: memory access (to read Rekordbox), Local Network (Govee), and Input Monitoring (Stream Deck).

The “Test the Lights” button

Replays a recorded session to drive the lights without Rekordbox running — so you can check a foreign rig before a set. It runs a preflight and refuses to start if Rekordbox is open, so it can’t collide with a real live bridge. Limit: recorded sessions live in /tmp today and vanish on reboot; a durable on-stick session is the next milestone.

What the host Mac needs

  • Rekordbox running in performance mode over the USB cable — the laptop is the real player.
  • [assumed, not confirmed as a written requirement] a matching Rekordbox version is likely needed, since the reader works off memory offsets that shift between versions.]

The hard limit

True standalone (no laptop) is impossible on the XDJ-RX3 — it has no Pro DJ Link. Performance mode on the laptop over USB is the way (that’s AWR-167; the standalone idea is shelved unless you get link-capable gear).
