## Who this DJ actually is (target everything at this)

- Library measured: heavy on **festival trap / bass flips and edits** — Knock2, ISOxo
  (ISOKNOCK), Crankdat, Ray Volpe, Juelz, Kai Wachi, Subtronics-adjacent — most of it
  **remixes/flips of recognizable pop songs** (Rihanna, Zedd, Usher, Britney flips etc.).
  Other half: **tech house** (Odd Mob, John Summit, FISHER, Chris Lake, Dom Dolla) plus some
  melodic (Rufus du Sol).
- Venue: a SMALL room — home/house-party scale with friends, low ceiling, short viewing
  distance. Not a festival, not a club. Consumer Govee RGB LED strips + MIDI-controlled
  lasers. No moving heads/blinders/CO2/video/haze.
- Rig is fully automated (no human on lights mid-set). Engine signals available: per-song
  measured character (grit/punch/bass/drama), Rekordbox key + drop/build/breakdown markers,
  live beat/BPM, deck faders.

## The three deep-dives

1. **His artists' actual shows.** Find show recaps, tour production breakdowns, LD/VJ
   interviews, and (highly valuable) fan video analyses for: Knock2 (nightclub / room2room
   era), ISOKNOCK, ISOxo alone, Crankdat, Ray Volpe, Kai Wachi, Subtronics. What do these
   specific shows do with color and lasers per song section? **Special focus: the pop-vocal
   flip moment** — this scene's signature move is a recognizable pop vocal that the crowd
   sings along to, followed by an original hard drop. How do their shows light the singalong
   part vs the drop? (Warm/soft for the vocal, then violence? Blackout on the vocal's last
   word? Anything sourced.) This matters because half this library is exactly that structure.
2. **Small-room translation.** Almost all LD lore is arena/festival-scale. Find credible
   material on lighting SMALL spaces: small-club (≤150 cap) lighting design, house-party /
   home-show setups, DJ livestream room lighting (a surprisingly professionalized niche —
   streamers light small rooms for cameras), low-ceiling rooms. Questions: appropriate peak
   brightness at short viewing distance (what's exciting vs blinding when the strip is 3
   meters from faces)? Does the pre-drop blackout still work in a room with ambient light
   spill? Do fast strobes read differently at close range? What do small-room designers say
   about restraint, fixture count, and negative space? Anything on making consumer strips
   look intentional rather than decorative.
3. **Laser design language.** Our rig has MIDI lasers and rounds 1–2 barely touched them.
   Research how bass-music shows use lasers (Excision's laser walls, liquid-sky effects,
   ISOKNOCK lasers) and how house/techno shows use them differently: When do lasers rest vs
   fire? What laser moves map to builds, drops, grooves, breakdowns? Laser color choice
   against the room's wash color (round 2 said complementary — confirm for lasers
   specifically)? Single-color vs multi-color laser moments? Small-room laser realities:
   what reads well at short distance without haze (beams need haze — what works without it:
   wall/ceiling scan patterns?). Safety-relevant staging conventions worth knowing (aim
   high, never at eye level) — practical, not regulatory boilerplate.
