## Target 1 — how specific genres produce different KINDS of lighting

Earlier rounds covered genre COLOR. This round: genre **motion, behavior, and production
style** — what the lights DO differently per genre, concretely enough to imitate:

- **Dubstep / riddim:** is the canonical drop treatment sustained full-rig strobing/stutter
  through the phrase? How do lights track the half-time feel? Headbang sections vs melodic
  interludes?
- **Techno:** driving, hypnotic, relentless — is the lore "tight beat-locked chases/pulses,
  minimal color movement, long unbroken patterns"? What do techno LDs do at a drop vs a
  breakdown (the famous restraint)?
- **Tech house / house:** groove-forward — rolling, swinging motion? What happens at a tech
  house drop specifically (e.g., a growl bar then groove: burst then settle)? How do lights
  swing WITH shuffle/groove vs ride straight quarters?
- **Bass house / electro:** stabby, punchy — do lights pulse/expand per hit? Loop patterns?
- **Festival trap:** halftime hits, 808s, big empty space — sparse hard hits with darkness
  between? Snare-roll builds?
- **Trance / melodic / progressive:** wash-driven, slow bloom, emotional peaks?
- For each genre: the drop treatment, the build treatment, the groove/verse treatment, and
  the breakdown treatment — as MOTION descriptions (what fixtures/washes/strobes DO), with
  sources (LD interviews, programming guides, show breakdowns, credible forum lore).

Context you should sanity-check rather than re-derive: the operator has specced four drop
archetypes — dubstep/trap → full-strip strobing sustained through the phrase; hard techno →
fast red beat-locked comet chase; tech house (growl bar → groove) → sparkle burst then
post-drop chase; bass house (stabby) → looping motion with aggressive on-beat
pulsate/expand. Confirm, refine, or challenge these against what pros actually do, with
sources.

## Target 2 — phrase lighting

EDM is built in 8/16/32-bar phrases, and the operator's bridge already tracks phrase
structure live. Research how professionals light PHRASES:

- Do LDs count bars/phrases, and how do they change looks at phrase boundaries (every 16?
  every 32? only at section changes)?
- **Intra-phrase development:** do pros add intensity/layers as a phrase progresses (bar 1
  quiet → bar 16 full), or hold steady per phrase and step at boundaries?
- Phrase-scale moves: 8-bar swells, phrase-end fills/accents (the lighting equivalent of a
  drum fill into the next phrase), one-bar "turnaround" moments before a boundary?
- Timecode shows vs busking: how does phrase awareness differ? Do busking LDs ride a
  "speed/size master" up through phrases?
- Anything on matching light-change RATE to phrase position (changes accelerating toward
  phrase ends)?
- How phrase lighting differs per genre (techno's long 32-bar arcs vs trap's 8-bar flips)?
