## 1. Artist signature languages

### Martin Garrix (confirmed core, via TPi/Robe; LD Gabe Fraboni)
- **The #1 stated rule: "never use the whole rig at once."** Each song is *defined by one
  dominant element* — some songs lighting-led, some video-led, some laser-led. Diversity by
  subtraction. (confirmed)
- Emotional/vocal tracks read serene/blue/cool/sustained; festival bangers read white-strobe +
  hard color + high movement. (reported)
- Brand palette is near-monochrome (black/white) + one signature accent (STMPD green);
  strobes used for *silhouette* and impact, blinder banks for crowd-facing drop hits;
  lasers reserved as a per-song identity element, not constant. (reported/synthesis)
- Rig-transfer: rotate which subsystem "owns" a track/section (strips vs lasers vs strobe
  hits), keyed by character + drop tier; composes with the existing drop-presentation ladder.

### Zedd (confirmed core; LD Steven Hernandez across True Colors/Orbit/Telos)
- **True Colors (2015) is the literal precedent for song-owns-color:** ten songs, ten fixed
  colors (Addicted to a Memory=purple, Straight into the Fire=orange, Bumble Bee=yellow,
  Transmission=black, Illusion=blue, Beautiful Now=magenta, Papercut=white, Daisy=green,
  Done With Love=teal, True Colors=red). White went to the orchestral-build track and red to
  the emotional-peak title track — white and red reserved for extremes. (confirmed)
- **Zedd's own stated mapping logic:** "a lot of reverb will always sound cold → blue…
  the drop is very energetic and aggressive, kind of like fire → orange." Artist-validated
  version of the smooth→cool / aggressive→hot axis. (confirmed, direct quote)
- Telos (2024-25): per-song lighting vibes off one central stained-glass arch; Orbit (2023):
  reviewers assumed timecode but the show is **run completely live** — tightness comes from
  operator anticipation, not pre-baked cues. (reported)
- Moment of Clarity (2013): finale = multi-color **laser tunnel engulfing the artist**. (confirmed)

### Alesso (confirmed core)
- **Mode-switch by section:** "colorful, slow-moving visuals for melodic breakdowns; glitchy,
  fast-cut graphics for heavy drops." Texture/speed of the visual tracks section type, not
  just color. (confirmed)
- The euphoric chord-progression lift is the *emotional peak*, accented every beat (strobe
  pulse/CO2 white plumes); the payoff is singalong euphoria, not violence. Light the lift
  itself, not just the drop. (confirmed)
- Rig-transfer: euphoric-melodic builds get rising brightness + beat accents landing on the
  downbeat; the white "cold-blast" analog fires once, on the hardest downbeat only.

### David Guetta (confirmed core)
- **Two temperaments, one artist:** mainstage anthem-pop (bright, warm, uplifting color
  explosion; lasers as texture/glitch accents — Louvre NYE rig: 116 Robe iPointes + 20×30 W
  lasers for "subtle highlights, disturbance and glitch") vs **Future Rave** (dark noir/
  galactic cold palette, strobes careening, lasers locked to basslines). (confirmed)
- Rig-transfer: the library naturally splits into these two temperaments on the existing
  smooth↔aggressive axis + violence tier — warm-bright treatment vs cold-dark-strobe
  treatment; keys off measured character, no per-track tuning.

### Knock2 / ISOxo / ISOKNOCK / Crankdat wave (mixed confidence; no dedicated LD interviews exist — honest gap)
- Knock2 nolimit "The Stack" (2025): retro speaker-wall; melodic = neon blue/purple washes,
  heavy = sharp reds/whites, strobes/lasers snapping discretely on-beat. (reported)
- ISOxo rejects the DJ-booth+LED-wall format (chains, CRT stacks, catwalk, live guitarist);
  ISOKNOCK 4EVR opened with silhouettes on a spark curtain. (reported)
- **Crankdat's laser philosophy, his own words: beams must pass over heads so people look UP
  — "Holy shit."** 360° stage thinking; ten 20 W lasers "pulsing perfectly with every drop."
  (confirmed quote)
- Rig-transfer: the above-head crossing volume is the hero zone (see §3); vocal-chop sections
  dip then snap back on the next hit (generalizes the round-3 vocal-flip rule beyond pop).

### Subtronics + the laser-designer craft layer (the deepest craft finds)
- Tesseract design (Erik Mahowald / Bending Lite): keywords "cosmic, ethereal, dimensional,
  quantum"; **sustained darkness/negative space as a compositional mode** — video content
  deliberately OFF for stretches so single-source lasers own a black room. (reported)
- **Laser Monkey (Bill Loftus — Illenium, Liquid Stranger, Red Rocks): two modes by genre.**
  Melodic = ONE slowly-morphing look sustained for minutes (the magic is inside the look).
  Heavy dubstep = timing over look design; **laser cue changes bind to snare-pattern changes**
  ("when the snare changes, the lasers also change with it") — fills/switch-ups, not clock.
  Thin/windy haze → simplify content, tighter/brighter beams. (confirmed)
- Corbin Sharpe (Levity): "I want the music to sound like it's painting the lasers"; every

  "moody blues and purples during deeper moments," "intense whites and reds when the energy

- **Cue on the anticipation point:** "pressing Go on the downbeat means your cue is already
  late" — pros fire a subdivision early so the change is *felt* on the beat. (confirmed; our

- **Hï Ibiza session-arc reveal:** a 10×10 fixture matrix hidden early in the night, revealed

- Summit himself: "dance fans are music-first… everything else is bells and whistles" —
  named-artist backing for restraint. (confirmed quote)

### Hard techno + era vocabularies
- **Red-for-hard-techno is the genre's own live look, independent of any track's measured
  smoothness** — Boiler Room "red-light vibe," I Hate Models' Dune palette ("scorched sunsets,
  deep reds, sandy CTOs, bold open whites"), "monochrome light rigs" recurring across festival
  descriptions. Independently corroborates the operator's AWR-147 red-divergence verdicts
  (Satisfaction/Age Of Love/Laserbeam/Stereo Love). (confirmed-to-reported)
- Warehouse strobe walls likely achieve intensity via **strobe DENSITY (multiple offset
  sources), not higher single-fixture Hz** — transferable: layer laser-strobe against strip
  flashes at offsets instead of raising any one channel's rate. (synthesis)
- One concrete pro number: strobe rate as a **ratio of BPM** (e.g. BPM/30 → 4 Hz at 120) —
  scales the same "feel" across tempo bands. (reported)
- **Era looks are distinct vocabularies — don't collapse them:** 90s rave = green lasers,
  slow organic scans, oil-wheel textures; 2008-12 French-electro = ONE white shape in a black
  room (Justice cross — the strongest historical precedent for "one shape, one color, total
  darkness"); Daft Punk's pyramid = pre-programmed choreography with live override (the
  direct historical analog of this bridge); modern hard techno = red/white monochrome blocks
  + strobe density. Era-flavor packs are viable but need an era/genre signal beyond BPM —
  flagged as a design gap, not a detector we have. (confirmed for Justice/Daft Punk; mixed
  for the rest; Gatecrasher/Cream trance-era palette UNVERIFIED — do not cite.)
- Gaps: Sara Landry / 999999999 / Trym / Héctor Oaks lighting specifics not findable this

## 2. What the perception science says about THIS room (dark living room, perimeter strips)

1. **Helmholtz–Kohlrausch effect (confirmed):** saturated color reads BRIGHTER than white at
   equal measured luminance — strongest for blue and red-magenta, weakest for yellow/green,
   and stronger in dark rooms. Saturated identity colors punch above their wattage here;
   white's advantage is physical output, color's advantage is perceptual. Both standing laws
   (white=wattage, color=identity) survive; this explains WHY they both work.
2. **Purkinje shift (confirmed):** as eyes dark-adapt, sensitivity slides toward blue-green;
   red washes out at low light. Low-simmer looks will read blue-biased; a dim red simmer is
   the weakest-reading choice — red wants LEVEL (or darkness-then-impact), matching "red is
   rare and earned."
3. **Afterimages (confirmed mechanism):** a hard white burst leaves a negative ghost for tens
   of seconds. Don't follow a white strobe burst with a delicate low look; follow with black
   (the ghost lives inside the blackout harmlessly) or with something bold.
4. **Blackout craft (confirmed + synthesis):** dark-adapted eyes detect near-starlight leaks —
   standby LEDs/screens become the whole scene once the strips cut; a genuinely clean room is
   what makes law-6 true black land. Sub-second cuts read as strobe-family punctuation;
   ≥1–2 s reads as a deliberate OFF state — consistent with the operator's darkness ladder
   (1–2-beat flicks vs 4/8/16-beat statements).
5. **Strobe rates (confirmed guidance, one honest note):** the 30 fps ceiling caps real
   strobes at ~15 Hz, which sits inside the 5–30 Hz photosensitivity risk band (15–20 Hz
   worst); venue guidance runs ≤4 Hz continuous with strobe-free recovery gaps, and bass-genre
   literature repeats the same 4 Hz convention. Current shipped default (6.0 Hz, 0.3 duty,
   burst-structured) is a taste/awareness item for the operator, not a mandate; darkness makes
   any strobe read MORE intense, so bursts-with-gaps matter more here than at a festival.
6. **Walls are filters (confirmed physics):** colored light on painted walls desaturates and
   hue-shifts on bounce; the diffusion/bounce hardware tip on record gets a physics reason.
   Test identity colors against the real wall color; wall-wash and direct-view are two
   different visual objects.
7. **Geometry as virtual fixtures (reported, adjacent research):** bias-lighting/Ambilight
   studies + wall-wash practice support treating single walls / corners / gradients as their
   own "fixtures" — asymmetric use of the perimeter reads more immersive than uniform rings.
   Direct research support for the operator's CONFIRMED-INTENT room-geometry workstream.

## 3. Lasers in the haze era (the new design space; rounds 1–4 assumed no haze)

1. **Vocabulary that survives authored patterns:** beam fans (the workhorse), fast-swept
   planes ("liquid sky" — a swept beam reads far brighter than a static spread line),
   tunnels/cones (compressed by a low ceiling — favor mid-height geometry), slow sweeps =
   tension, fast scans = release. Free-form graphics/logos need true vector drawing — out.
2. **Haze craft:** light/even haze = crisp defined beams; heavy = volumetric soup that dims
   contrast. Small rooms: short bursts then stop; haze lingers and density CREEPS UP over a
   set — feed it less over time. Design every look to read in light haze so nothing depends
   on soup. (confirmed ILDA + community)
3. **The mirrored pair's unique payoff (synthesis on confirmed physics):** beams don't
   interfere physically; where the two identical fans cross in haze you get bright
   intersection nodes and a **woven lattice / X-canopy a single laser cannot make** —
   crossing above heads makes that volume the hero zone (Crankdat's look-UP moment).
   Wall-terminated sheets from opposite walls read as two bundles; center-crossed reads as
   one canopy.
4. **7-color physics:** green ≈5–7× brighter per watt than red (eye peak ~555 nm); cyan rides
   green's sensitivity and reads electric; red ≈24% relative brightness — red beams need a
   DARK room to read (fine: our red moments come with darkness); white fires all diodes (max
   cost — reserve for peaks); blue reads deep/moody rather than piercing (right for smooth
   zones). Existing zone→accent pairs survive contact with the physics.
5. **Color-speed channel:** static = a stated mood; slow cycle = breathing; fast cycling =
   cheap — reserve for extreme-tier chaos if ever. Laser strobe = discrete impact accent only.
6. **The balance law — darkness is the laser's stage (confirmed):** ambient wash kills beams.
   When beams fire, the strip wash should dip/darken that instant; drama can be produced by
   DARKENING the wash (raising accent:ambient contrast toward 10–15:1) instead of brightening
   the beam. Small-room bonus: wall bounce makes modest lasers read dense. This mechanism IS
   the operator's charge-discharge reframe (strips feed the lasers, drop unleashes) — direct
   research support for creative proposal #1.
7. **Snare-change trigger (confirmed craft):** heavy-genre laser cue changes bind to
   snare-pattern changes (fills/switch-ups) — maps cleanly onto F4 texture containment
   (variant selection inside an already-chosen moment, never new timing authority).
8. **Melodic mode:** one slowly-morphing sustained look held for minutes beats cutting —
   extends "boring is fine, one strong look" to lasers on smooth tracks.

## 4. Color psychology & ambiance levers (residential scale)

1. **Saturation is the violence knob; brightness is the euphoria knob (confirmed):**
   saturation drives arousal (high saturation can tip toward menace — right for violence
   tiers, wrong for calm); brightness drives alertness + positive lift. Map: violence tier →
   saturation ceiling; euphoric lift → luminance ramp. Independent levers.
2. **Warm shrinks the room, cool expands it (reported, converging):** warm/low-CCT reads
   intimate/enclosed (strongest in the dark); cool reads spacious/open — and hue shifts felt
   temperature. Composition tool: teal breakdown = the walls blow out; warm afterglow = the
   room hugs. ALLEYCVT's cool→warm build ramp exploits exactly this.
3. **Chromatic adaptation (confirmed, with numbers):** a dead-static uniform color fades
   perceptually (slow phase τ≈40–160 s; full fades ~10–20 min); motion/structure refreshes
   it. Never hold a truly static wash past ~1–3 min — breathe it (F1's planned breathing is
   validated). Free weapon: the color change AFTER a long hold reads hyper-vivid.
4. **Simultaneous contrast (confirmed):** the wash defines what the laser color READS as —
   accent choice must be relative to the current wash (the existing complementary-accent
   zone pairs are the right shape).
5. **Anticipation science (confirmed — the marquee fact):** dopamine release peaks during
   ANTICIPATION, anatomically distinct from the peak-pleasure response (Salimpoor; Huron's
   ITPRA). Reactive products can only decorate the post-hit moment; only a plan-ahead system
   can light the pre-drop window — which is the neurologically potent one. This is the
   scientific one-liner for why this bridge's whole F2 approach is the right bet.
6. **Peak-end + scarcity (confirmed):** nights are remembered by the best moment and the
   ending; rationed extremes imprint hardest. Folds into the PARKED SET-mode extension notes
   (operator verdict #5) — night-level look budgeting, the finale guarantee. No new workstream.
7. **Leitmotif (confirmed):** recurring look for a recurring anthem builds recognition in
   seconds — F1's permanent identity is the mechanism; optional refinement: a second play the
   same night may "remember louder" (slight embellishment, same identity).
8. **Faint floor glow between peaks (confirmed bias-lighting research):** a dim non-black
   simmer stabilizes pupils across hours AND makes designed blackouts read deeper — aligns
   with the standing ruling (breakdowns ride sparse-and-dim; true black reserved for the
   blackout window). Validation, not a change.

## 5. The landscape — what nobody else does (confirmed survey)

Hue Sync / LedFx / WLED-SR / Nanoleaf 4D / TouchDesigner rigs: all REACTIVE (late by
definition, post-hit only), identity-less (same song looks different every play), memoryless
(no night arc, no scarcity). SoundSwitch AutoScript is the closest competitor — offline
phrase/drop detection — but assigns generic preset cues by fixture role: no per-track
identity, no measured violence tiers, no scarcity/memory. **The intersection this bridge
occupies (permanent identity + measured per-drop tiers + true plan-ahead + phrase grid +
incoming stems) is empty in the market.** (confirmed product-behavior survey)
